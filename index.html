About

Most good ideas never leave the group chat.

Zura Hack is where they finally get built. Teams form, ideas turn into working products, and by the end of the event everyone has something real to show.

You'll build alongside people who ship, get guidance from mentors who have done it before, and leave with new teammates, new skills and a project you're proud of.

How it works

From idea to demo.

Here's what your Zura Hack journey looks like, from signing up to taking home a prize.

  1. 1

    Join the waitlist

    Get on the list so you're first to know when registration opens.

  2. 2

    Register and form a team

    Sign up solo or with friends. Teams can be formed at the event too.

  3. 3

    Kickoff

    Meet the mentors, hear from sponsors and get the tracks and rules.

  4. 4

    Build

    Heads down. Mentors check in with every team to help you get unstuck.

  5. 5

    Demo and judging

    Show what you built to the judges and the room.

  6. 6

    Awards

    Winners are announced, prizes are handed out and the celebration starts.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Still curious? Email hack@zura.com.ng or send us a DM on X.

Who can join?

Anyone who builds or wants to: developers, designers, founders and students. Beginners are welcome.

Do I need a team?

No. You can come with a team or join one at the event. We'll help solo hackers find teammates.

I'm a beginner. Should I still come?

Yes. Mentors will be around to help, and joining a team with mixed experience is one of the fastest ways to learn.

What should I build?

Anything useful. Pick one of the tracks, or bring your own idea. Sponsor tracks will be shared before the event.

Can I start building before the event?

Come with ideas, sketches and research, but write your project during the hackathon so every team starts on equal ground.

When and where is it?

We're announcing the date and venue soon. Join the waitlist and follow @zuraOSS on X so you don't miss it.

How can my company get involved?

Email hack@zura.com.ng. We offer headline, track and community partnerships, plus mentoring and speaking slots.

Ready to build?

Spots will be limited. Get on the waitlist and be the first to know when registration opens.
