Why sponsor

Reach builders

Put your product in the hands of developers and designers while they decide what to build with.

Hire talent

Watch people work under real pressure and meet the ones you'd want on your team.

Get feedback

See how builders use your API or product, and hear what they'd change.

Grow your brand

Show up as a company that invests in the local tech community, online and in the room.

Sponsor FAQ

Common questions.

Can we sponsor with credits or products instead of cash?

Yes. Credits, data, food, swag and hardware are all welcome, and in-kind sponsors get logo placement and shoutouts too.

Can our team mentor or judge?

Yes. Track sponsors judge their own track, and any sponsor or partner can send mentors.

Can we recruit at the event?

Yes. Headline and track sponsors can connect with hackers who opt in to share their details.

Can we run a workshop?

Track and headline sponsors get a workshop or demo slot. It's a great way to get builders using your product on the day.

Is there a custom package?

Yes. Email us with your goals and budget and we'll build one around them.
